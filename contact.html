Module 6 JavaScript Project

Contact Me

This contact form is part of my Module 6 project. It uses JavaScript to validate your information while you type and provide immediate feedback before the form can be submitted.

How the Form Works

Each field is checked using JavaScript. Your name must contain letters, your email must follow a valid email format, and your message must contain at least 10 characters.

If something is incorrect, a message appears directly below the relevant field. The form cannot continue until all three fields are valid.

When every field passes validation, JavaScript prevents the normal form submission and displays a confirmation modal containing the information entered.

This is a course project. The information entered into this form is not sent to a server.
